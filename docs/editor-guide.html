1
The sixty-second version If you read nothing else, read this page. It is enough to change any piece of wording on the website.
  1. Open your editor. Go to the link on the cover of this guide and sign in with the email address your invitation was sent to.
  2. Pick where the words live. Down the left-hand side you will see five things. Pick the one that matches what you want to change — Section 4 of this guide tells you which is which.
  3. Click the box and type. The page becomes a simple form. Click into a box, change the words, exactly as you would in an email.
  4. Press Save. That is it. There is no publish step and nothing else to press.
  5. Wait about a minute, then reload your website. Your change is live. If it is not there yet, wait another minute and reload again.
The one habit worth having

Change one thing, press Save, then look at the website. Once you trust it, do bigger edits. Every save is stored separately and any change can be undone later, so nothing you do is permanent.

Why there is no publish button

Saving and publishing are the same action here. When you press Save, your website is rebuilt automatically. That is why there is a short wait before you see the change — the website is being reassembled, not just updated.

The three questions everyone asks first

Can I break it? Not the design. You are given boxes to type in and menus to pick from — there is no way to type something that breaks the layout. See Section 14.
Do I need to know code? No. You will never see any. If you can fill in a form, you can run this website.
What if I make a mistake? Type it back and press Save again. If you cannot fix it, every previous version is kept and can be restored — see Section 15.

Logging in You do not need a GitHub account, a password to remember, or any software installed.
  1. Find your invitation email. It comes from Pages CMS. If you cannot find it, check your spam folder before asking for another.
  2. Click the link in the email. This proves who you are. You will not be asked to create a password.
  3. Bookmark the page you land on. That is your editor, from now on.
Signing in again later

Go to your bookmark, type the same email address, and a fresh sign-in link is emailed to you. This is called a magic link — the email is the password, which is why there is nothing to forget.

Use the same email address every time

Your access is tied to one address. Signing in with a different one will not show you the website, even if both inboxes belong to you.

What you will see

The editor has a list down the left-hand side and a form on the right. Clicking something on the left opens its form on the right. There are no other screens, no settings to configure, and nothing that can be switched on or off by accident.

What the website is made of Five kinds of page. Knowing which is which tells you where to go to change something.

How they connect

  • The home page introduces you and links to the three gallery pages.
  • Each gallery page — Design, Photo, Film — shows a grid of cards.
  • Each card leads to a project page, where the full set of images lives.
The useful consequence

A project's card on the gallery page and its own page are the same project in your editor. Rename a project once and both update, because the card is built from the project, not typed separately. The same goes for the Next project links at the bottom of each project page — they look after themselves.

Nineteen projects, mostly empty

Eighteen of the nineteen are still placeholders reading "Project 01" and "Project description to follow." That is deliberate — they were left blank rather than filled with invented clients. Fill them in as real work is ready. Section 12 walks through one.

Changing wording The simplest job, and the one you will do most.

Worked example: your email address

  1. Click Site-wide in the list on the left.
  2. Find the box labelled Contact email address.
  3. Replace what is there with the new address.
  4. Press Save, wait about a minute, and reload the website.

Every CONTACT NOW button, every Contact menu item, every Contact me button and the footer address have all changed — across all twenty-four pages.

Headline lines, and why they are a list

The big contact headline is three separate lines that stack. They are three boxes rather than one so the line breaks always fall where the designer intended. Add a fourth line if you want, or remove one — the layout copes.

About the two "Search-engine description" boxes

These do not appear on the page itself. They are the grey summary line Google shows beneath your page title in its results. Around 150 characters is right. If you leave one alone, nothing breaks.

Typing symbols is fine

Ampersands, quotes and accented characters are all handled for you. Type "Fish & Chips" and it appears correctly. You cannot accidentally type something that damages the page.

9
Swapping a photo Every image on the website works exactly the same way, wherever it appears.
  1. Find the image. Use Section 5, 6 or 7 to work out what it is called, then go to that place in your editor.
  2. Click the image box and choose your file. You can drag a photo onto it, or pick one you have uploaded before.
  3. Fill in "Describe the image". One short sentence saying what is in the photo. This is not optional — see the note below.
  4. Check the two shapes and two crops. Section 10 explains these. If you are unsure, leave them as they are and look at the result first.
  5. Press Save, wait a minute, reload.
Why "Describe the image" matters

It is read aloud to blind visitors using screen readers, and it is what appears if the photo ever fails to load. Google reads it too. Write what the picture actually shows — "AK photographing a model against a white wall", not "photo" or "image1".

The placeholders currently say things like "Placeholder for Design Project 01". Replacing those is one of the most valuable things you can do.

Why there are separate settings for phones

A wide photo that looks great on a laptop is a thin strip on a phone. Most images on this website are set to Wide on desktop and Tall on phones, so they stay generous on a small screen. That is why there are two of each box, not one.

Shapes and cropping The only part of this guide worth reading twice.

Your photo is never squashed or stretched. It is placed in a frame and the edges are trimmed away. These two settings decide the frame's shape, and which part of the photo survives.

Shape — the frame

Keep this part in frame — the crop

Same photo, same frame, three different choices. The dotted line is the whole photo; the solid box is what visitors see.

Your six choices

Centre · Upper middle (good for faces) · Top · Bottom · Left · Right. Every image on the website is currently set to Centre, so if a new photo looks badly cropped, this is the first box to try.

Why you cannot type an exact position

These are a fixed menu rather than a free box on purpose. It means you can never enter something the design cannot handle. If you genuinely need a crop that none of the six give you, that is a two-minute job for your developer.

11
Changing a film Every film on this website is a YouTube link. You never upload video.
  1. Put the film on YouTube and copy its link from the address bar, or use YouTube's Share button.
  2. In your editor, open Projects, choose the project, and find the YouTube video block in its Project gallery.
  3. Paste the link into "YouTube link". Paste the whole thing — you do not need to trim it or find an ID.
  4. Fill in "Video title". The film's name. Used by screen readers and as the player's title.
  5. Upload a still into "Still image". This is the picture visitors see before they press play. See the note below.
  6. Save, wait a minute, then reload and press play to check it.

Links that work

All of these are fine — paste whichever you have:

youtube.com/watch?v=q38Be7Plti4The normal address bar link
youtu.be/q38Be7Plti4?si=…The Share button link
youtube.com/shorts/…A Short
youtube.com/live/…A stream
…watch?v=q38Be7Plti4&t=42sA link with a start time — the extra bits are ignored

If you paste something that is not a YouTube link, the block quietly shows just your still image with no play button, rather than showing visitors a broken player.

Why you choose the still yourself

Nothing at all loads from YouTube until a visitor actually presses play. That keeps your site fast and means Google is not tracking your visitors on your behalf. The trade-off is that YouTube's automatic thumbnail is not available — so you pick the frame, which is better anyway, because you can choose a good one.

Export a frame from the film at around 1280 × 720 and upload it as the Still image.

Adding a film to a project that has none

Every project can have a film — not just the Film ones. In the Project gallery, add a block and choose YouTube video. It works identically anywhere.

12
Filling in a project page Turning "Project 01" into a real piece of work.
  1. Open Projects and click the one you want. They are listed by title, so the empty ones still read "Project 01" through "Project 06".
  2. Project title. The real name. This also updates its card on the gallery page and the tab title in the browser — you only type it once.
  3. Project description. The paragraph at the top of the page. Replace "Project description to follow."
  4. Project details. Rows of label and value. Two rows are there now, Client and Services, both saying "To be added". Change them, add rows such as Year or Role, or remove them entirely — the layout adjusts to any number.
  5. Gallery card. The image representing this project on the Design, Photo or Film page. Often your strongest shot rather than the first one.
  6. Project gallery. The images down the page. Add, remove and drag blocks into any order you like.
  7. Save, wait a minute, reload the gallery page and the project page.

The four gallery blocks

BlockUse it for
One full-width image A single picture across the full width. Your best shots.
Two images side by side A pair, shown together on a computer and stacked on a phone. Good for before-and-after or details.
Pull quote A short line of text set to the right — a client quote or a note about the work. A sentence or two at most.
YouTube video A film, as described in Section 11.
Reordering is drag and drop

Blocks can be dragged up and down. Any order works, and any mixture works — six full-width images, or a film followed by three pairs, or a single image and a quote. The design handles all of it, because each block is styled independently.

What updates by itself

The project's gallery card, its position on the gallery page, its browser tab title, and the "Next project" link at the foot of the page. None of these are typed anywhere. This used to be four separate edits kept in step by hand.

Photo sizes One number to remember.

Export photos about 2,000 to 2,500 pixels wide before uploading. JPG or WebP.

A photo straight off a modern phone is often 4,000 pixels wide and 8 megabytes. Your website never displays it larger than about 1,500 pixels, so the extra is downloaded by every visitor and then thrown away. On a phone on mobile data, that is the difference between a site that feels instant and one that feels slow.

Full-width images2,000–2,500 pixels wide
Portraits and paired images1,200–1,600 pixels wide
Film stills1,280 × 720
FormatJPG, or WebP if your software offers it
If you are not sure how to resize

On a Mac, open the photo in Preview, then Tools → Adjust Size, and set the width to 2400. On a phone, most photo apps offer a "Medium" or "Large" option when sharing, rather than "Actual Size".

What happens if you upload a huge one anyway

Nothing breaks and the photo appears correctly. The page is simply heavier than it needs to be. It is worth getting right, but it is not worth losing sleep over.

14
What you cannot break — and what to leave alone Worth reading once, so you can edit with confidence.

Things that are genuinely impossible

  • You cannot break the layout. Shapes and crops are fixed menus, not typing boxes. There is no way to choose a value the design cannot handle.
  • You cannot take the website offline. If a save ever fails to build, the previous version stays live. The worst case is that your change does not appear.
  • You cannot lose an old version. Every save is kept, forever, individually.
  • You cannot damage the styling. Colours, fonts, spacing and the scrolling effects are not exposed to you at all.
  • You cannot delete a page. Adding and removing project pages is switched off deliberately.

Things to leave to your developer

The business card page The page behind your NFC card. It has its own design and is tied to a contact file that has to match it. Ask, do not edit.
DESIGN. CREATE. INSPIRE. The huge statement on the home page. It contains typographic instructions about where the line may break, so it is not offered as an editable box.
Your AK SUGGI wordmark Your logo, effectively. Fixed in the design.
Adding or deleting whole projects Currently switched off. It can be switched on in one line whenever you want it — just ask.
The Instagram link Set in the code at the moment, so it needs a developer. Worth asking to have it moved into your editor.
The thinking behind all this

You have been given every box that changes what visitors read and see, and none of the boxes that change how the site is built. That is why you can edit freely without needing to be careful.

15
When something goes wrong In order of how often it actually happens.

My change has not appeared

Wait two minutes and reload. If it still is not there, force your browser to fetch a fresh copy: Cmd + Shift + R on a Mac, Ctrl + Shift + R on Windows. Your browser keeps a local copy of the site to make it fast, and sometimes shows you that instead.

I uploaded the wrong photo

Upload the right one over it and Save. No need to delete anything.

The photo is cropped badly

Section 10. Try Keep this part in frame first, then Shape. Remember to check the phone settings as well as the desktop ones.

It looks wrong on my phone but fine on my laptop

Almost always the two phone-specific boxes: Shape on phones and Keep this part in frame (phones). They are separate settings by design.

I edited the wrong project

Type the original wording back and Save. If you cannot remember it, ask your developer to restore that version — it takes a minute and nothing is lost.

The film will not play

Check the film is public on YouTube, not private or unlisted. Then check the link is pasted in full. If the play button has vanished entirely, the link was not recognised as a YouTube address — paste it again from the address bar.

I want it back exactly how it was

Every save is stored separately and any one can be restored. Tell your developer roughly when the change happened and what it was.

What to send when you ask for help

The address of the page, what you expected, and what you got instead. A screenshot showing the whole browser window is worth several paragraphs.
